Our Story

Deccan Brew Café is a fictional neighbourhood café imagined for this portfolio project. It blends the warmth of old Hyderabad chai culture with the calm of a modern coffee house.

We picture a cosy space for long conversations, quiet reading and a good cup in your hand.
